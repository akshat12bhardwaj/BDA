# Assumption Register

Only assumptions that actually exist as of the current phase are listed. Each will be revisited and either confirmed, revised, or retired as later phases execute.

| ID | Assumption | Reason | Source | Affected Phase | Impact | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-01 | The 320 non-empty mandi commodity filenames are a reasonable proxy for "crop name" and can be matched (after normalization) against APY `crop_name` and Agri Market `Commodity` values. | Filenames were manually curated per commodity by the dataset publisher; no separate crop-ID field exists in the mandi files. | Direct inspection of mandi folder (Phase 1) | 2, 4 | Determines which crops are even eligible for cross-dataset scoring. | Exact-string matching already shown to under-count true overlap (Phase 1, Section 11) — a normalization/mapping step is required, not yet built. |
| A-02 | `Arrivals (Tonnes)` (mandi and Agri Market) represents supply-side quantity reaching the market, not consumer demand. | Column name and unit (Tonnes) are consistent with a physical goods-received measure; no dataset documentation or field defines a demand concept. | Direct inspection of both datasets' schemas (Phase 1) | 4, 6, 7, 9 (future) | Any future "market activity" or "demand" model must be built and labeled as an arrivals/traded-quantity proxy, never true demand. | If a genuine demand signal is later found, this assumption should be revisited. |
| A-03 | `final_data.csv` is a market/district/state code reference table intended for ID standardization, not a source of price/production/weather data. | Its only columns are names and codes for State/District/Market; no numeric measurement columns exist. | Direct inspection (Phase 1) | 4 | Its use is limited to join-key harmonization; it will not contribute predictive features. | Its exact upstream source/publisher is not stated in the file itself and has not been independently verified. |
| A-04 | Agri Market Dataset coverage (Maharashtra/Amravati only, 5 crops, 2020–2024) will be used only as a regional validation/cross-check signal, not a mandatory input for every selected crop. | Making it mandatory would force crop selection toward a geographically narrow, non-representative subset. | Direct inspection (Phase 1) | 2 | Crop selection can proceed using mandi + APY + weather-feasibility as the primary criteria. | If a selected crop happens to fall outside Maharashtra/Amravati, no market-activity cross-validation from this source will be possible for it. |
| A-05 | Mandi `Reported Date` values can be safely parsed using `pandas.to_datetime(..., format="mixed")` across the two formats observed (`DD Mon YYYY` and ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`). | Both formats were directly observed in sampled files (Phase 1, Section 4); `format="mixed"` correctly parsed the sampled Onion.csv rows with 0 unparsed dates. | Direct inspection + test parse (Phase 1) | 2, 4, 5 | Enables uniform temporal analysis across files without per-file format branching. | Not yet verified across all 320 files — only a sample and one full file (Onion.csv, 200k rows) were tested. Phase 2/4 should verify at full scale. |
| A-06 | The 5 empty mandi files (`Jaggery`, `Ladies Finger`, `Nargasi`, `Saffron`, `Seegu`) are genuinely empty (data-collection gaps at source) and not caused by a read error on our side. | Their line count is exactly 1 (header only) via direct file read, consistent across repeated checks. | Direct inspection (Phase 1) | 2 | These 5 are excluded from the crop-selection candidate pool. | Not independently verified against the original data publisher; assumed to reflect the source data as-is. |

| A-07 | APY `Rice` production statistics are agronomically closer to mandi `Paddy(Dhan)(Common)` (paddy/unmilled output) than to mandi `Rice` (milled market product), but the two mandi commodities are kept fully separate and neither was merged with APY `Rice` in Phase 2's shortlist. | Rice and Paddy are traded as distinct commodities with different prices in Indian mandis; merging them would misrepresent the data. | Domain knowledge applied during Phase 2 crop-name normalization review (`Code/utils/crop_matching.py:MANUAL_ALIAS_NOTES`) | 2 (documented, not selected) | If Rice/Paddy is reconsidered in a future scope change, this distinction must be preserved. | Not independently verified against an official APY methodology document beyond the codebook's general description. |
| A-08 | APY `Gram` is treated as equivalent to mandi `Bengal Gram(Gram)(Whole)` only "with caution" (not a certainty) — this was a contributing reason Bengal Gram was not selected despite reasonable mandi coverage. | APY `Gram` most commonly refers to Bengal gram/chickpea in Indian agricultural statistics, but this is a convention, not a guarantee that no other gram variants are included in APY's figure. | Domain knowledge applied during Phase 2 crop-name normalization review | 2 (documented, not selected) | Not applicable — Bengal Gram was not selected. | If revisited, this alias should be re-verified against the codebook/source methodology before use. |
| A-09 | Zero-price rows, min>max-price rows, and duplicate rows found in Onion/Potato/Wheat (Phase 2 audit) are assumed to be genuine data-quality issues in the source data, not artifacts of the audit's own reading/parsing logic. | The same `load_mandi_file()`/`parse_dates_fast()` utilities were validated against known-good samples in Phase 1/2 before being applied at scale; the anomaly rates (roughly 0.3–5% of rows depending on the check) are consistent with typical government administrative data quality. | Phase 2 feasibility audit (`Processed_Data/crop_feasibility_mandi.csv`) | 5 (cleaning) | Determines the cleaning approach Phase 5 will need to justify (treat vs. retain each anomaly type). | Not cross-verified against an independent copy of the source data. |

| A-10 | A district's representative weather coordinate (its principal town, as returned by the Open-Meteo Geocoding API) adequately represents weather conditions across that entire district for this project's purposes. | Weather does not vary sharply enough within a typical Indian district to justify per-mandi-level resolution, and no per-mandi coordinate source exists anyway. | Phase 3 design decision (Decision Log D-11) | 4, 10 | Determines the spatial resolution of any future weather feature. | Could understate real intra-district weather variation, particularly in geographically large or topographically varied districts. |
| A-11 | The top-20-by-mandi-volume districts per crop are a reasonable representative sample of each crop's weather-relevant geography, even though they only cover ~15–20% of that crop's total mandi records. | High-volume districts are more likely to be the ones a future price/supply forecasting or optimization phase will actually focus on; the long-tail remainder consists of many low-volume districts. | Phase 3 finding (top-20 coverage share computed in the Phase 3 notebook, Section 7) | 4, 10 | Weather features will only be available for these representative districts; low-volume districts will have no weather data unless a future phase extends collection. | Not verified that low-volume districts are systematically similar in climate to their state's high-volume districts. |
| A-12 | The 10 unresolved geocoding locations (Section "Unresolved Locations" in `Documentation/Weather_Data_Collection.md`) are due to spelling/naming mismatches between the mandi dataset and the geocoder's gazetteer, not genuine ambiguity about which real-world place is meant. | The mismatches follow a recognizable pattern (e.g. "Bhatinda" vs official "Bathinda", "Ganganagar" vs official "Sri Ganganagar") consistent with informal transliteration variants common in Indian administrative data. | Manual review of the 10 unresolved cases during Phase 3 | 4 | If confirmed, a small manual alias table could resolve most of them in a future phase. | Not independently verified against an authoritative gazetteer; left unresolved rather than acted upon in Phase 3. |

| A-13 | APY `year` labels (`YYYY-YYYY`) denote India's July–June agricultural year, so a mandi date maps to `apy_year` = the July–June year containing it. | The label format spans two calendar years and matches the agricultural year used in GoI agricultural statistics. The codebook only says "Year", so this was not verified from an in-project document. | Phase 4 inspection of APY + domain convention (Decision Log D-19) | 4, 6, 10 | Determines which APY year a mandi record is linked to. | Definitional: stored Rabi produce sold after June is labelled with the next agricultural year. If APY used a different year definition, links would shift by up to one year. |
| A-14 | The reviewed district aliases in `Code/integration/district_aliases.csv` (spelling variants, official renames, headquarters-town names) refer to the same district as the APY name. | Each alias has a stated basis, and every target is checked to exist in APY. Ambiguous cases (splits, reorganisations) are excluded rather than guessed. | Phase 4 review (D-18) | 4, 6, 10 | About 20% of records are linked to APY through an alias. | Based on documented domain knowledge, not cross-checked against an external gazetteer. Districts created by later splits may cover different areas in early years. |
| A-15 | The Open-Meteo geocoder's `admin2` field correctly identifies the district a candidate place lies in. | It is the geocoder's own administrative metadata and agreed with the expected district for 45 of 46 locations (the 46th was the wrong-district Phase 3 point it exposed). | Phase 4 verification (D-16) | 4, 10, 11 | Decides which coordinates are accepted for weather and future routing nodes. | Not checked against official district boundary polygons. |

| A-16 | A zero min/max/modal price in the mandi data means "not reported", not a sale at Rs 0. | Zero-price rows almost always have positive arrivals; no row has all three prices zero; every min > max case disappears once zeros are missing. | Phase 5 analysis (D-27) | 5, 6, 8 | ~5% of min/max and 0.5–1.2% of modal prices become missing. | Strong internal evidence, but not confirmed by the data publisher. |
| A-17 | Price ratios ≥ 50× and arrivals ratios ≥ 100× (against the trailing references in D-29/D-30) are data-entry or unit errors rather than real market events. | They match unit-conversion factors (×10, ×100, ×1000) and lie far outside the observed dispersion. | Phase 5 analysis (D-29, D-30) | 5, 6, 8, 9 | These rows are excluded from the usable views; values kept. | Heuristic; a rare genuine extreme event could be excluded, and smaller entry errors are not caught. |
| A-18 | Conflicting records (same key, same arrivals, different prices) cannot be resolved from the available data. | No grade, lot or timestamp field exists in the source to tell versions apart. | Phase 5 duplicate investigation (D-26) | 5, 6 | 338 rows are kept but excluded from the usable views. | If a later source supplies grade/lot information, these could be resolved. |

| A-19 | APY figures for agricultural year Y are published within about 6 months after Y ends, so year t−2 is always available during agricultural year t and year t−1 is not reliably available. | The codebook's "Data Last Updated 14-12-2023" release already contains 2022-23 (which ended June 2023). | Phase 6 (D-36) | 6, 8, 10 | APY features use lag 2. | Based on a single observed release date; publication timing in earlier years is unknown. |
| A-20 | A prediction for a record dated t is made with information available up to the end of day t−1 (one-step / next-reporting-day setting). | The project does not yet fix the forecast horizon (Roadmap Phase 8 anticipates 7–30 days). The strictest safe row-level cutoff is "strictly before t". | Phase 6 (D-34) | 6, 7, 8, 9 | All history features use only data dated < t. | For an h-day horizon, features must be taken from the forecast-origin row or recomputed with a t−h cutoff; using them directly would leak h−1 days. |
| A-21 | IMD meteorological seasons (winter Jan–Feb, pre-monsoon Mar–May, monsoon Jun–Sep, post-monsoon Oct–Dec) are a reasonable seasonal descriptor for weather-related variation. | They are the India Meteorological Department's standard seasonal definition. | Phase 6 (D-37) | 6, 7 | Gives a documented seasonal categorical feature. | They are not crop harvest seasons and do not claim to be. |

| A-22 | Nominal Rs/quintal prices are adequate for Phase 7 description and for seasonal comparison when each price is expressed relative to its own series-year median. | No price deflator exists in the project; within-year ratios cancel the year's general price level. | Phase 7 EDA (`seasonal_index.csv`) | 7, 8 | Seasonal and volatility findings do not depend on inflation; long-run level trends do. | Rupee-level trends across decades mix real change and inflation; a deflator would be needed for real-price statements. |
| A-23 | APY district values for agricultural year Y1–Y2 are usable from 1 July of Y2 + 1 (`apy_lag2_policy_v1`), so a decision in agricultural year t uses source year t − 2 only. | It restates the D-36 lag with an explicit date, at least 12 months after the year ends. The one observed release (A-19) came about 6 months after the year ended. | D-47 (Phase 10) | 10, 12, 13 | `available_from` and `information_rule` on every Phase 10 row; the leakage checks in the Phase 10 tests. | **A policy assumption, not a verified or universal publication schedule.** No district-level release history is verified. Secondary, unverified metadata indicates late coverage of recent years, so the rule is not shown to be conservative for source years ≥ 2019-20. The input is one 2023 vintage, so earlier years carry revised values. |
| A-24 | A single "Whole Year" APY row (`whole_year_row`) and a single named-season row (`single_named_season`) are not verified annual totals. Only a Total row (`verified_total`) is. | Total rows equal the sum of the seasons (D-20). DES has not stated what "Whole Year" covers for seasonal crops, and a single Rabi or Kharif row may be partial. | D-46, D-48 (Phase 10) | 10, 12 | The four comparability classes are never merged; consumers must state which they accept; R9 annotates single named seasons for all crops. | If DES confirms that "Whole Year" is the full-year total, that class could be read as annual, but only under a new decision. |

**Status updates (Phase 4)**: A-05 is confirmed at full scale for the selected crops (all 7,983,133 Onion/Potato/Wheat dates are `DD Mon YYYY`, 0 unparsed). A-12 is confirmed: all 10 unresolved locations were spelling or naming variants and are now resolved with admin2 evidence (D-16). A-04 is refined: Agri Market Wheat values are identical to national mandi records where both exist, so it is a key-consistency check, not independent validation (D-23).

**Status updates (Phase 8 planning)**: A-20 is refined by D-38. Phase 6 row features keep their t−1 cutoff and are reused unchanged. Phase 8 forecasts are made at the end of a Monday–Sunday week for target weeks 1, 2 and 4 weeks later, using only rows dated on or before that origin (`Phase_08_Modelling_Plan.md` §B).

**Status updates (Phase 10)**: A-19 is unchanged, as D-47 requires. A-23 states the Phase 10 availability rule explicitly as a policy assumption. A-13 still underlies the July–June year keys. A-14's caveat that districts created by later splits may cover different areas in early years remains open, because the boundary rule R8b is blocked.

No assumptions have yet been made about transport cost, storage cost, warehouse capacity, or market capacity — these will be added in the phases that actually require them (Phase 11+), not fabricated here in advance.
